Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding plot transfers, construction bylaws, and costs.

What is included in the Grey Structure package? ↓
Our Grey Structure package (PKR 2,400–2,800/sq.ft) includes soil testing, foundation excavation, anti-termite chemical membrane, Mughal Supreme Grade 60 steel, certified first-class Kalat brickwork, Maple Leaf / DG Khan cement, plaster on all walls, complete concealed electrical conduits (pipes & boxes), water supply (PPRC) and drainage (PVC) lines, underground and overhead water tanks, and boundary walls.
How long does it take to construct a 5 Marla or 10 Marla house? ↓
A standard 5 Marla double-story turnkey house typically requires 6 to 8 months from map approval to keys handover. A 10 Marla residence requires approximately 8 to 10 months, while a 1 Kanal designer bungalow typically takes 10 to 12 months, ensuring proper curing times for concrete and plaster.
How does MR Real Estate ensure title deed authenticity for plots? ↓
Manzar Malik personally conducts a three-point verification: (1) Direct physical record check at the DHA / Bahria / LDA transfer office, (2) Verification of No Demand Certificate (NDC) and clearance of all development charges, and (3) Physical on-ground site demarcation to verify plot peg markers and road width.
Can Overseas Pakistanis buy property or build without coming to Pakistan? ↓
Yes! Through the Pakistan Embassy / Consulate, you can execute an Attested Power of Attorney (POA). We guide you through every step of DHA/Bahria requirements. For construction, stage payments are tied strictly to milestones verified by video call and ledger reports.
